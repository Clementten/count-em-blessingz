Four ways to ask

All four keep the app's promise: nothing is gated and nobody is nagged.

Build this

A. Tip jar in Settings

A "Support" group with one row, "Feed the developer". It opens a sheet with five fixed amounts, bought as consumable in-app purchases.

  • Fits Apple's rule for tipping the developer
  • SGD is the base price; other currencies follow Apple's own price points
  • Near-zero code, no server
Add to A

B. One quiet moment

After someone shares their Year in Review, a single line appears under the card with two buttons. It shows once per year, and not at all in a year they have already tipped.

  • Asks when the app has just delivered value
  • Once a year, never on launch
  • One local flag
Include

C. Fortune seal, in each Vibe's colors

After any tip, offer a red-chop style 福 (fortune) seal on the Year in Review card. It stamps in once. Cosmetic only, off unless the user ticks it.

  • Gives thanks a visible form
  • Local flag, no entitlement check
  • Gold on Jade & Gold, crimson and gold on The Grand Ledger, bronze on Quiet Fortune
Avoid

D. Link to Ko-fi, DuitNow or PayNow

Pointing out of the app to take money for the developer risks rejection under guideline 3.1.1 in most storefronts.

  • Confirm the current wording before relying on it
  • Keep all giving inside the app, through Apple

Clickable prototype

Real theme colours and fonts from Themes.swift. Tap rows and tiers inside the phone, or jump with the controls.

Screen
Vibe

Placement. Between Gratitude and Data, so it sits near the end of Settings and never on Home.

Tone. The app's own vocabulary: blessings, not donations.

Honesty line. The sheet says plainly that nothing changes in the app after a tip.

Prices are shown in Singapore dollars for this preview only. In the app, the App Store shows each price in the user's own currency.

Accessibility

  • Tiers are real buttons, at least 44pt tall
  • VoiceOver reads "Tiny blessing, 99 cents, button"
  • Dynamic Type: tiers wrap, sheet scrolls
  • Reduce Motion: envelope swaps without animation

Privacy

  • No analytics, no receipt upload, no identifiers
  • Only the Apple purchase sheet touches the network
  • Listing line "no network code" needs rewording

Not included

  • No locked features, no Pro tier
  • No custom amounts (the App Store does not allow them)
  • No reminders to donate
