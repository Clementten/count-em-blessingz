Header Art Options

One Universal asset for the product page header and the search results. 16:9, 5244 × 2950 px, PNG, no transparency. Five concepts below, drawn on the real canvas. The dashed box is Apple's art safe area (1402 × 962 px). Anything that matters stays inside it.

What gets someone to download, and to stay

The asset has about a second. It also sets an expectation, and the app has to meet it.

Understood in one second

People decide fast, and clutter loses them. One image and one line that says what the app is for.

"That's for me"

A red envelope is instantly recognised by the people who give and receive angpao. Naming the occasion (angpao, duit raya) makes the right person feel seen.

Trust with money

A list of who gave what is personal. Saying "private, no account" removes the fear that stops people installing.

Retention starts here

Promise only what the 1.0 app does. A mismatch between the art and the app is the fastest way to a delete. The thank-you list and year-in-review are what bring people back each season.

Rules from Apple that shape every option: no prices or discounts, no website addresses, no copyright symbols, no award claims, and it must read as 4+. The tip jar and fortune seal are not in 1.0, so they do not appear.

My pick

Go with A for the header

It wins on attracting people: instant, warm and recognisable to exactly the people who give and receive angpao. The rest of the listing (screenshots, description) already covers trust and features.

Keep E as the runner-up
