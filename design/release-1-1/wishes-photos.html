Wishes and Photos Preview

Clickable preview of two changes: blessings that are wishes or prayers with no amount, and photos that show a tag, open full screen and can be saved. Everything below is a prototype. It is not the app.

Try this: on Home, tap the photo tag on a row to open the preview. Tap the "+" button, switch to "Wish or prayer", and press Save with nothing filled in to see the checks.

1.0 behavior: a photo was only a 64 pt thumbnail inside the editor. Nothing in the list showed it, and nothing opened it.

Prototype only: the photo is a drawing, the share sheet is a mock, and the entries reset when you reload.

What changes

Wish or prayer New

  • A switch at the top of the editor: Money or Wish or prayer
  • No amount and no method for wishes
  • The wish text is required, or a photo of a card
  • List row shows a sparkle where the amount would be
  • Money totals, charts and the widget ignore wishes

Photo tag Fix

  • Any entry with a photo shows a small photo mark before the date
  • The mark is its own 44 pt button
  • Tapping the rest of the row still opens the editor
  • Works in Home and in a person's profile

Preview and save Fix

  • Full-screen preview from the tag or from the editor thumbnail
  • Double tap or pinch to zoom, swipe down to close
  • Share opens the system sheet, which includes Save Image
  • Save puts it straight in Photos, with a clear message if permission is off

Hall of Fame

  • Ranked by money only
  • People who only gave wishes are listed below the ranking
