# Count 'Em Blessingz support

Need help? Email **sherlock10holmes@hotmail.com** and say which iPhone and iOS version you use. Please do not email your data files unless asked to.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. The app has no account and no cloud. If you lose or reset your phone, the data is gone unless you made a backup, so back up regularly: **Settings > Data > Back up to file**, then keep the file somewhere safe (for example Files or iCloud Drive).

### How do I move my data to a new phone?
Restoring from a device backup brings the app back with its data. Otherwise, on the old phone choose **Settings > Data > Back up to file** and send the file to yourself, then on the new phone open it and choose **Settings > Data > Restore from backup**. Restoring **replaces** everything on that phone.

### How do I combine lists with a family member?
Each person records on their own phone. To combine:
1. On one phone, tap the share button on the Home screen (or **Settings > Data > Share all my entries**) and send the file with AirDrop, WhatsApp, Telegram, Mail or Messages.
2. On the other phone, tap the file and choose Count 'Em Blessingz (or use **Settings > Data > Import from file**).
3. Review the list. Entries you already have are locked, possible duplicates are left unticked, and people with similar names are never merged unless you say so. Tap **Import** when you are happy.

Nothing changes until you tap Import, and **Settings > Data > Undo last import** puts your pot back for 7 days. Imports only add or update entries; they never delete anything.

### Why are some entries unticked as "possible duplicate"?
They have the same person, the same amount and a date within three days of an entry you already have, so they might be the same gift recorded twice. Tick them if they are really separate gifts.

### Can I change my currency?
Yes: **Settings > Country and currency**. The app asks for an exchange rate so your amounts (and your goal, if you set one) convert. You can also keep the numbers and change only the label. Your original amounts are remembered.

### What does the Face ID lock do?
It locks the app when you leave it and hides its contents in the app switcher. While it is on, the home screen widget also hides your total.

### Why did my thank-you reminder not appear?
Check that notifications are allowed for the app in the iPhone Settings app, and that **Reminder delay** in the app's Settings is not Off. Reminders are scheduled a few days after each blessing, only for people you have not marked as thanked.

### How do I hide amounts when someone is looking?
Tap **Hide amounts** on the Home screen. You can also turn on **Hide amounts by default** in Settings.

### How do I delete everything?
**Settings > Data > Erase all data** (it asks twice), or delete the app.
