# Privacy Policy for Count 'Em Blessingz

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

**In short: Count 'Em Blessingz keeps everything on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data, and the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.**

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores the information you type in: the names of people, amounts, occasions, dates, notes and any photos you attach, plus your settings (country, currency, look, goal and reminder choices). This information is kept **only on your device**, in the app's private storage and in a container shared with the app's home screen widget so the widget can show your total. We never see it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not make any network connection. It has no servers, no account system and no cloud sync.
- It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software that collects data.
- It does not track you, and it does not use your data to build a profile.
- It does not read your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

## Photos

When you attach a photo, you pick it with the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose and keeps a smaller copy inside the app. It does not browse your photo library.

## Face ID and passcode

The optional app lock uses the system's Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. Your biometric data stays inside iOS; the app is only told whether the check succeeded.

## Notifications

Thank-you reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They mention the name of the person you want to thank. If you hide notification previews on the lock screen, iOS shows a generic message instead of the name. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Sharing and backups

Data leaves your device only when you ask it to:

- **Share buttons** open the standard iOS share sheet with a file containing your entries (names, amounts, notes and photos). It goes to the app or person you choose, such as AirDrop, Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, Mail or Files. We are not involved, and what those services do with the file is covered by their own policies.
- **Export and backup** create a file you save or send yourself.
- **Safety copies before an import.** Before you merge a file from someone else, the app keeps up to three copies of your data on your device so you can undo the import. They are removed automatically after a few imports, and they are excluded from device backups.
- **Device backups.** iOS may include the app's data in your iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer), according to your own settings. We have no access to those backups.

## Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in **Settings > Data > Erase all data**, or by deleting the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can also turn off reminders, the Face ID lock and amount display in Settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
